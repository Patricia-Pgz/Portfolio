About Me

I’ve always been fascinated by the logic and complexity behind games, so I packed my bags, moved 400 km, and dove into a Games Engineering degree.
Alongside my studies, I spent several years gaining hands-on experience — about two years developing interactive 3D web visualizations with Angular, and over three years creating game-like VR simulations for psychological research in Unity.
Through these projects, I handled 2D/3D asset creation and integration, collaborated with researchers and developers, and even trained colleagues along the way. I’ve also gained deep experience with multiple game engines, frameworks, and programming languages — all the way up to developing my own C++/OpenGL game and engine from scratch.
I love learning new things and turning ideas into meaningful, engaging experiences for players. I’m excited to find a team where I can put my skills and motivation to work, grow as a developer, and help create something truly special.

Game Projects

Electron X Pulse

This project showcases my work with C++ and OpenGL. (Click the image above to watch a short trailer)
As part of the final stage of my studies, I developed a 2D side-scrolling platformer with an integrated level editor and a custom engine framework designed for rapid creation of similar games. The gameplay is synchronized to the beat through automatically analyzing audio and adjusting the editor grid to beats as units for the designer.
The engine features key systems such as Rendering, Physics, Audio Analysis, UI, State Management, ECS, Level Loading, and a Level Editor.

ACP Game

My bachelor’s thesis focused on the conceptualization and prototyping of an Advance Care Planning (ACP) serious game. I designed and developed a 3D clue-search game in Unity aimed at raising awareness about ACP — the process of expressing one’s values and wishes for future medical treatment in case of becoming unable to decide for oneself — targeted specifically at young adults.
Guided by direct feedback from medical experts, I designed an engaging experience that closely simulates real-life scenarios. Visual (shader) and audio effects as well as cutscenes were used to convey emotions, guiding the storyline and emphasizing the importance of the subject.

Runecraft

A 3D fantasy VR game using the Unity Game Engine. The player takes up the role of a wizard, drawing runes to select spells and overcome their enemies. Contribution: All level designs, as well as UIs and some gameplay systems. [Teamproject, Assets taken from Unity Asset Store]

The Doors of Perception

The Doors of Perception is a Unity plugin, for game designers to easily set up and trigger multiple perception states with audio, visual(shader) or custom effects, and transitions between them. We additionally created different demo VR environments to visualize application of the tool for game development. [Teamproject]
